Our Work

Case Studies

Atlas Design System

A multifaceted project including a scalable design system built with responsiveness and theming at its core.

Atlas includes a component-based UI kit, a token system that translates directly to CSS variables, and centralized documentation—ensuring consistency and streamlining collaboration across design and development teams.

BroadFutures

A complete brand refresh using inclusive principles tailored to the neurodivergent community.

The engagement included a comprehensive style guide and website to support consistent, neuroinclusive communication, along with participatory design sessions to align on strategy and refine brand direction.

QuantFu UI Kit

A cohesive UI system and product experience for a complex trading platform.

The work focused on reducing cognitive load through consistent components, clear patterns, and an assistant-style “bot” that helps users automate and navigate trading workflows. The result is a streamlined product experience that makes advanced trading more approachable and efficient.

NeuroX

A comprehensive initiative integrating brand identity with a thoughtfully designed, neuro-inclusive conference experience.

Features such as quiet rooms, cognitive-supportive resources, and social battery cards allowed every attendee to engage comfortably, embodying the “carving your own path” concept.
